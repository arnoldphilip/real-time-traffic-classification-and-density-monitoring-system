1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To effectively validate and demonstrate the 3-Camera Coordinated Traffic Signal
Optimization algorithm, you need 3 distinct video footages representing the 3 
arms of an intersection. 

For the YOLOv8 vehicle detection, centroid tracking, and PCU calculation to work
optimally, the AI-generated videos must satisfy these surveillance criteria:
- FIXED CAMERA ANGLE: High-angle static pole-mounted CCTV perspective (no panning,
  no camera shaking, no zoom).
- CLEAR VEHICLE MORPHOLOGY: Distinct cars, buses, trucks, and bikes with clear
  separation and sharp silhouettes.
- STOP LINE & ROAD LANES: Visible road asphalt, lane markings, and an approach
  corridor leading up to an intersection stop line.

The 3 videos are structured to trigger the full range of the dynamic algorithm:
- Camera 1 (Arm A): HEAVY CONGESTION (High PCU -> Triggers Max Green ~50-55s & Priority)
- Camera 2 (Arm B): MODERATE TRAFFIC (Medium PCU -> Balanced Green ~25-35s)
- Camera 3 (Arm C): LIGHT / EMPTY FLOW (Low PCU -> Clamped to Minimum Green 15s)

================================================================================
RECOMMENDED AI VIDEO GENERATION PLATFORMS & SETTINGS
================================================================================
1. Kling AI (klingai.com):
   - Model: Kling 1.5 or 2.0 (High Quality mode)
   - Mode: Text-to-Video
   - Duration: 5s or 10s (generate 2-3 clips and concatenate, or set to loop)
   - Camera Control: Set Camera Movement to "Static" / None.

2. Runway Gen-3 Alpha (runwayml.com):
   - Mode: Text to Video
   - Motion: Set Motion scale to 3 or 4 (moderate, steady vehicle movement)
   - Camera Motion: Disable all camera paths (keep fixed).

3. Luma Dream Machine (lumalabs.ai/dream-machine):
   - Append to prompt: "static tripod CCTV perspective, fixed angle, photorealistic security camera"

4. Pika Labs (pika.art) / Sora:
   - Parameters: `-camera zoom none -camera pan none -camera tilt none -fps 30`


================================================================================
HOW TO LOAD THE 3 AI VIDEOS INTO THE SYSTEM
================================================================================
Method A: Direct 3-Video Upload (Fastest)
   1. Start ATCC:
      streamlit run unified_app.py
   2. Navigate to "🚦 Signal Optimizer" in the sidebar.
   3. Choose "📁 Upload 3 Video Footages".
   4. Upload:
      - Video 1 (Heavy) -> Footage 1
      - Video 2 (Moderate) -> Footage 2
      - Video 3 (Light) -> Footage 3
   5. Click "🚀 Analyze 3 Video Footages & Optimize Signal".
   6. Watch the algorithm dynamically compute the PCU loads and assign:
      - Cam 1: 52s Green (Priority Active)
      - Cam 2: 28s Green
      - Cam 3: 15s Green (Minimum baseline)

Method B: Live Feeds Matrix Integration
   1. Place the generated MP4 files in your project or upload to any video host / YouTube.
   2. Under "📹 Live Feeds", click "➕ Add Live Camera".
   3. Add all 3 feeds.
   4. Open "🚦 Signal Optimizer", select "📹 Select from Live Camera Feeds", and run!
